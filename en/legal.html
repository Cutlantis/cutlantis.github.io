Privacy

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from third-party services — fonts included. The host (GitHub) keeps technical logs (IP address, date, page requested) for security.

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which receives your name, email address and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details, for invoicing and support.

The software processes videos on your computer: no video, transcript or clip is uploaded, and no usage statistics are collected. It only connects to:

  • YouTube, to download the video you ask for;
  • SponsorBlock, to spot sponsored segments — only the first 4 characters of a hash of the video ID are sent;
  • PyPI, the official Python package index, once a day, to update the module that downloads from YouTube;
  • Lemon Squeezy, to activate your licence and then check at most once a week that it is still valid: the key and an anonymous computer identifier (a hash, never its name) are sent.
