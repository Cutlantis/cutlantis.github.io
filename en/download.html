If something goes wrong

"This key is already active on the maximum number of computers"

Open Cutlantis on the old computer and click "Deactivate on this computer" under your licence. No longer have the old computer? Write to us.

A YouTube download fails

YouTube changes often; Cutlantis updates its YouTube module on its own, once a day. Quit and reopen the app; if it persists, write to us.

Sending us the log

Mac: Help menu → Show log. Windows: the file %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cutlantis\logs\cutlantis.log. Attach it to your message: it shows us what happened.

Uninstalling

Mac: drag Cutlantis to the Trash; your projects stay in Library › Application Support › Cutlantis. Windows: Settings → Apps → Cutlantis → Uninstall; your projects stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cutlantis. Delete that folder to erase everything.
